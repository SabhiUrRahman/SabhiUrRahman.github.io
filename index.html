01Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. I'm in Lahore (UTC+5) and work remotely with clients worldwide, with a few hours of overlap for calls.

02Can my team edit the site after launch?

Yes. I build with clean classes, components and CMS collections, then record a short handover video so your team can edit pages, CMS items and forms without breaking the layout.

03How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, the CMS and the motion. After a short call I send a fixed quote with the scope and the timeline, so there are no surprises.

04How long does a build take?

It depends on the scope. You get a timeline with the quote, and a live staging link from day one so you can watch the site come together.

05What's in a no-code security check?

A passive review of your live Webflow or Framer site: security headers and CSP, forms and spam protection, custom code and third-party scripts, exposed keys, and who has access to the project. You get a short report with fixes in priority order.
